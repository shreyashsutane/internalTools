Client-Side Cryptographic Policy

This utility uses **CryptoJS** to execute **AES-128-CBC** encryption and decryption with **PKCS7 padding**. Key and IV strings are parsed dynamically through UTF-8 word conversion, conforming exactly with standard C/Java valid bytes padding logic (copied and padded/truncated to exactly 16 bytes). No sensitive key, IV, or payload data is ever transmitted over the network.
